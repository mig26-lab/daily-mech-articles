# Avride Reaches One Million Autonomous Robot Deliveries Across the United States

**Published: November 15, 2026**  
**Category: Robotics / Delivery / Logistics**

![Sidewalk delivery robots lined up outside a store](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2023/11/12/15/34/robot-8384012_1280.jpg)

*Image: Multiple delivery robots on urban street from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License – free for commercial use with attribution preferred)*

Avride announced on November 15, 2026, that its autonomous sidewalk delivery robots have completed one million deliveries across the United States. The milestone reflects rapid scaling in urban neighborhoods and university campuses, with the company reporting 180,000 deliveries in October alone and peak daily volumes of 7,000.

## Operations and Partnerships

Avride now operates in more than 30 U.S. markets, including Austin, Dallas, Jersey City, Philadelphia, and Miami. Its robots integrate with major food-delivery platforms such as Uber Eats and Grubhub, so customers order through familiar apps. Restaurant staff load orders into the robots, which navigate autonomously to destinations. Customers track progress and unlock cargo compartments via the partner applications.

The company attributes growth to coordination among engineering, manufacturing, operations, and commercial partners. Recent expansions include a deal with Sodexo Campus covering roughly 425 U.S. institutions and deeper integration with Wonder across multiple cities.

## Why the Milestone Matters

One million deliveries indicate that sidewalk robots have moved beyond limited pilots into routine last-mile service for short-distance food and goods transport. In dense urban and campus settings, the robots can reduce vehicle trips and labor costs for repetitive short hauls. Daily volumes of several thousand show operational reliability at meaningful scale.

Avride also operates robotaxi services in select cities (including rides on Uber), but the delivery milestone focuses on the sidewalk fleet. The company plans continued expansion while refining experiences for restaurants, campus dining teams, and end customers.

## Broader Context in Autonomous Delivery

The achievement arrives as other players expand drone and sidewalk networks. It underscores that commercial viability for last-mile robots depends on dense demand corridors, reliable navigation, and seamless platform integration rather than pure technological novelty.

## Key Takeaways

- One million cumulative robot deliveries; 180,000 in October; up to 7,000 daily.
- Active in 30+ U.S. markets via Uber Eats, Grubhub, and campus partners.
- Customers track and unlock robots through existing delivery apps.
- Signals maturation of sidewalk robot logistics for urban and campus use.

## Helpful Tips

- For restaurants and campuses: evaluate robot options in high-density, short-distance zones for cost and reliability gains.
- Track partner app integrations—most users never leave their preferred delivery platform.
- Monitor weather and infrastructure constraints that still limit outdoor robot operations.
- Compare with emerging drone networks for longer or aerial last-mile segments.
