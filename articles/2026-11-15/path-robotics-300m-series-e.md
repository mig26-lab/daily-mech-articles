# Path Robotics Raises $300 Million at $2.45 Billion Valuation to Scale Physical AI for Manufacturing Welding

**Published: November 15, 2026**  
**Category: Robotics / AI / Funding**

![Robotic arm in industrial setting](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2023/07/22/03/37/ai-generated-8142626_1280.jpg)

*Image: Industrial robot illustration from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License)*

Path Robotics, the Columbus, Ohio-based developer of physical AI software for autonomous manufacturing robots, has raised $300 million in Series E financing at a $2.45 billion post-money valuation. The round, led by Georgian, nearly quadruples the company’s previous valuation and brings total funding to over $610 million. The capital will accelerate expansion of its welding platform and extension into additional manufacturing tasks and robotic form factors.

## Funding Details and Growth Metrics

Announced on November 10, 2026, the Series E includes participation from existing investors Addition, Drive Capital, IronArc Ventures, Tiger Global, and Yamaha Motor Ventures, plus new backers D. E. Shaw Ventures and NAVER Ventures. Georgian partner Emily Walsh will join the board.

Annual recurring revenue has grown 17-fold over the past 18 months as deployments scaled across major North American industrial customers. Headcount has risen 97% year-over-year, with a target of 400 employees by year-end. Customers include HII, Lockheed Martin, and Saronic; a notable $600 million performance-based agreement with HII supports U.S. Navy shipbuilding under the High-Yield Production Robotics program.

## Technology and Applications

Path’s Obsidian physical AI model enables robotic systems—including arms and quadrupeds adapted from platforms like Boston Dynamics—to perform complex welding on variable parts. The system uses computer vision and machine learning to identify joints, plan paths, and adapt in real time, addressing challenges such as heat-induced distortion that have historically limited automation of skilled welding.

The company is expanding beyond welding into other high-value manufacturing processes critical to industrial capacity, including defense, energy, and heavy equipment. Founders Andy and Alex Lonsberry have emphasized that the technology adds capacity rather than simply displacing workers.

## Market Context and Outlook

This raise fits a broader wave of physical AI investment, alongside humanoid and industrial automation funding. Success depends on translating welding wins into broader factory automation while navigating long industrial qualification cycles. With strong commercial traction and defense contracts, Path is positioned to help address skilled labor shortages in manufacturing.

## Looking Ahead

Watch for scaled deployments, new task expansions, and potential further capital or strategic partnerships. The valuation reflects confidence in physical AI’s transition from pilot to production-scale revenue.
