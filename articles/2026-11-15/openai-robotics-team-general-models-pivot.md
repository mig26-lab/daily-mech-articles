# OpenAI Disbands Dedicated Robotics Team, Betting General Models Can Drive Robots

**Published: November 15, 2026**  
**Category: AI / Robotics / Strategy**

![Futuristic humanoid robot](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/06/12/06/06/robot-8824475_1280.jpg)

OpenAI has reorganized its robotics efforts, folding the standalone robotics team into broader research groups under leaders including Mark Chen and Greg Brockman. The company is betting that frontier general-purpose models—such as GPT-6 Astra—can handle robot control tasks without specialized robotics training pipelines. Early results show GPT-6 Astra completing a subset of StationeryBench tasks and navigating kitchen environments on humanoid platforms, outperforming some specialized baselines on progress metrics.

## Strategic Shift

Historically, OpenAI maintained a dedicated robotics group exploring embodied AI. The reorganization reflects a view that rapid advances in large multimodal models reduce the need for robot-specific architectures. By applying general models directly, OpenAI aims to leverage its core training and inference infrastructure for physical tasks. This contrasts with approaches from Google DeepMind (Gemini Robotics VLA models) and others that still invest in specialized vision-language-action systems.

## Early Performance Signals

Reports indicate GPT-6 Astra achieved an average progress score of 46 on a limited StationeryBench subset (7 of 100 tasks completed), compared with lower scores from competing embodied models. Kitchen navigation demos on humanoids further suggest that general models can transfer language and vision understanding into basic physical actions. These results remain early; full reliability, long-horizon planning, and fine dexterity still require additional work.

## Industry Context

The move arrives alongside Google DeepMind’s Gemini Robotics 2.5 (96% success in Mercedes-Benz parts kitting), Embodied AI’s AIR factory humanoid, and continued humanoid pilots from Apptronik, Agility, and Chinese firms. It also follows OpenAI’s launch of ChatGPT Wallet for agentic commerce, signaling a broader push toward agents that act in both digital and physical domains. Some observers see the reorganization as a resource reallocation toward foundation models, while others view it as recognition that embodiment can emerge from scale.

## Risks and Opportunities

Opportunities include faster iteration by reusing existing model infrastructure and potential cost advantages if general models reduce the need for large robot-specific datasets. Risks include slower progress on hard dexterity and safety problems that specialized robotics teams have traditionally prioritized. Safety evaluation for physical actions remains an open challenge, especially as models gain more real-world agency.

## Outlook

OpenAI’s pivot tests the hypothesis that sufficiently capable general models can serve as robot brains. Success would accelerate deployment by lowering barriers to physical AI; limited progress would reinforce the value of dedicated robotics research. Through 2027, comparative results between general-model control and specialized VLA systems will clarify which path scales more effectively for industrial and service robotics.
