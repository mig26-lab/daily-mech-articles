As of mid-November 2026, Anthropic is in the final stages of its historic IPO roadshow, aiming for a valuation of up to approximately $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis. The company behind Claude has set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares, with trading expected to begin on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19. The book has been reported as oversubscribed by as much as 6x, signaling strong institutional demand despite broader questions about AI spending sustainability.

## Key Terms of the Offering

Anthropic launched its roadshow around November 10 after filing an amended S-1. At the top of the range, the primary offering is projected to raise up to about $64.35 billion. A concurrent private placement of roughly $26 billion in non-voting Class N shares to cornerstone investors—including Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, and TSMC—brings the total capital raised potentially above $90 billion when including underwriter options.

Run-rate revenue has been cited near $81 billion as of late October, with substantial compute commitments approaching $900 billion. Cornerstone participation from major chip and cloud suppliers underscores the interdependent ecosystem.

## Market Context and Demand Signals

The valuation sits below earlier rumors of $1.5–2 trillion but still represents a massive step up from private rounds around $965 billion earlier in 2026. Reports indicate the institutional book covered early and that demand has remained robust, with some speculation about pricing above the range. Retail allocation is expected to be higher than typical IPOs (around 20%). Pricing is anticipated on November 18 ahead of the November 19 debut.

Broader AI sector volatility, including leadership changes at NVIDIA and questions about return on massive infrastructure spend, makes this listing a key test of Wall Street’s continued conviction in frontier AI labs.

## Implications for the Industry

A successful Anthropic listing would provide a public benchmark for Claude’s competitiveness against models from OpenAI, Google, and others. It also locks in capital for continued training and deployment while highlighting the capital intensity of the field. Public shareholders will gain visibility into financials, safety commitments, and competitive positioning.

For developers and enterprises, the IPO does not change near-term product access but signals long-term resource availability for Claude’s agentic and coding capabilities.
