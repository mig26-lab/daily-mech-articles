# BYD and MOGOX Unveil 8.5-Metre Level 4 Autonomous Electric Bus for International Markets

**DailyMech Staff** · 15 November 2026

*Photo by falco on Pixabay — Pixabay Content License*

![Autonomous electric shuttle](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2020/01/12/07/03/vehicle-4759347_640.jpg)

**BYD and Beijing-based autonomous driving company MOGOX (Zhidao Network Technology) jointly unveiled the B90, an 8.5-metre right-hand-drive Level 4 autonomous electric bus, at Singapore Urban Mobility Week 2026. The global debut marks a significant step in factory-fitted autonomous public transit designed for efficient adaptation across different markets.**

## Key Specifications and Design

The B90 is built on BYD’s established electric bus platform and integrates MOGOX’s proprietary MOGO AutoPilot system. It features a low-floor layout and ultra-short rear overhang, improving manoeuvrability on narrow urban roads and facilitating easier boarding for elderly passengers and people with reduced mobility.

The autonomy stack combines cameras with solid-state LiDAR, localised specifically for left-hand traffic and right-hand-drive configurations such as those in Singapore. The system is engineered to handle mixed traffic, intersections, and approaches to bus stops while supporting long-duration operations.

MOGOX emphasises modular adaptation: the company splits market customisation into seven reusable modules covering regulations, vehicle platforms, fleet management, and local support. This approach aims to reduce repetitive development work when deploying in new countries.

## Singapore as Launchpad

Singapore serves as MOGOX’s primary gateway for international expansion. The company secured the contract for Singapore’s first official Level 4 autonomous bus project and has passed the Land Transport Authority’s Milestone 1 (M1) test. Earlier plans involved a pilot on services 191 and 400 with a consortium that includes MKX Technologies and BYD Singapore, though some deployment details have evolved.

The vehicle is positioned as a series-built product rather than a retrofitted pilot, signalling a shift toward more scalable autonomous public transport.

## Broader Implications

This collaboration highlights the growing role of Chinese electric vehicle and autonomy specialists in global public transit markets. BYD gains a factory-integrated autonomous offering for export, while MOGOX leverages Singapore’s rigorous certification as a credential for other high-specification markets. Partners mentioned in related announcements include ComfortDelGro Bus in Singapore and others in South Korea and Luxembourg.

The debut underscores ongoing progress in Level 4 autonomous buses moving from controlled trials toward practical urban deployment.
