Anthropic rolled out Claude Meetings in beta on November 12, 2026, for Team and Enterprise plans. The feature allows Claude to join Google Meet calls as a visible, named participant that follows the conversation in real time, answers questions, takes notes, and collaboratively builds documents, whiteboards, and other artifacts while the meeting is still underway.

## How Claude Meetings Works

Users connect Google Calendar in Claude and add Claude to a scheduled meeting (or enable auto-join). Once in the call, Claude appears as a participant and posts a notice in the chat. By default it responds in writing. Participants can ask questions out loud or in chat; with permission it can use connected tools.

A key capability is live artifact creation. Saying “Claude, start a doc for this plan” or “Claude, make a whiteboard” produces a shared document or board that teammates can edit during the call. Notes, action items, and transcripts are generated automatically. Support is currently limited to Google Meet, with other platforms planned.

During the beta, notes and most in-call answers are included at no extra charge. Artifact creation remains free through February 10, 2027. Certain organizations (zero data retention, HIPAA-ready, or customer-managed keys) are excluded from the initial rollout.

## Why This Matters for Knowledge Work

Most meeting AI tools activate after the call ends. Claude Meetings shifts the workflow earlier: ideas discussed on the call can be turned into structured outputs before the meeting concludes. This reduces the gap between discussion and execution and lowers the burden of post-meeting note-taking and follow-up.

It aligns with broader moves toward agentic AI that participates in workflows rather than merely summarizing them. Combined with Claude’s coding and reasoning strengths, the feature supports planning, documentation, and light tool use inside collaborative sessions.

## Availability and Limitations

- Plans: Team and Enterprise (on by default for Team; admin-controlled for Enterprise).
- Platform: Google Meet first.
- Data: Retains transcripts, summaries, and screen descriptions rather than raw audio.
- Exclusions: ZDR, HIPAA-ready, and CMEK configurations.

Early coverage highlights the potential for higher meeting productivity while noting that success depends on clear prompting and organizational governance around AI participation.

## Key Takeaways

- Claude joins Google Meet as a visible participant in beta.
- Live notes, Q&A, docs, and whiteboards during the call.
- Available on Team and Enterprise plans starting November 12, 2026.
- Artifacts free through early February 2027 during beta.

## Helpful Tips

- Start with a clear invitation phrase such as “Claude, draft a project timeline on a whiteboard.”
- Review organizational settings to control auto-join and external participant behavior.
- Treat outputs as collaborative drafts—edit them live rather than waiting for a final summary.
- Check data retention policies if your organization has strict compliance requirements.
