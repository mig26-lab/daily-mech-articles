# Pony.ai and Toyota Launch Fully Driverless Gen-7 bZ4X Robotaxi Service in Guangzhou

![Autonomous vehicle interior view](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2026/08/05/21/48/21-48-20-729_640.jpg)

*Image from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License.*

Pony.ai and Toyota have begun fully driverless commercial robotaxi service in Guangzhou using their jointly developed seventh-generation bZ4X vehicle. The launch, announced around November 15–16, 2026, marks a concrete step from prototype and testing fleets into paid, no-safety-driver operations with a mass-produced electric platform.

## Key Details of the Launch

- **Vehicle**: Gen-7 bZ4X Robotaxi, collaboratively engineered by Pony.ai and Toyota. Features Bluetooth unlocking, intelligent voice assistant, in-car entertainment, and personalized climate controls. Built on a Toyota platform with redundant systems designed for Level 4 operation.
- **Service area and hours**: Guangzhou (initially noted in areas such as Nansha), available 24 hours a day. Passengers hail via the PonyPilot app or mini-programs on WeChat and Alipay.
- **Scale target**: Partners are working toward roughly 1,000 bZ4X robotaxis across major Chinese cities. This is a deployment target; current operational numbers in Guangzhou are not fully detailed in initial announcements. Testing and preparation continue in other cities.
- **Partnership history**: Collaboration dates to 2019. First mass-produced Gen-7 bZ4X units rolled off the line in February 2026. A joint venture and investment (previously reported around RMB 1 billion) supported development, production, and operations.
- **Capabilities claimed**: Handles heavy rain, nighttime driving, and congestion according to company statements. Pony.ai already operates robotaxi services in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen with other vehicles.

## Broader Context in Autonomous Mobility

China remains the global leader in commercial robotaxi deployment. Multiple operators now run fully driverless services in several cities. The Toyota partnership brings established vehicle manufacturing, reliability engineering, and supply-chain scale to Pony.ai’s autonomous stack. This contrasts with pure software-first approaches and may accelerate cost reduction and fleet reliability.

Pony.ai has also reported strong growth in paid robotaxi revenue and is expanding internationally (including partnerships and pilots in Europe). The bZ4X entry adds a purpose-built, mass-produced platform to its fleet mix.
