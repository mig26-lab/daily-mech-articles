# Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO: Colette Kress Steps In as Interim Leader

**Published: November 14, 2026**  
**Category: AI / Tech / Business**

On November 12, 2026, Nvidia announced that founder and long-time CEO Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO, and board member, effective immediately. After 33 years at the helm of the company he co-founded, Huang's departure marks a significant transition for the chipmaker that has become synonymous with the AI boom.

CFO Colette Kress was appointed interim CEO while continuing in her finance role. The board also named Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever chair. Nvidia is initiating a search for a permanent successor, considering both internal and external candidates.

## Background of the Transition

The resignation followed revelations of extramarital affairs, including one involving a businesswoman US authorities reportedly identified in connection with Chinese intelligence efforts. Nvidia's board launched an independent investigation. Reports indicated a second relationship with a direct report. Huang publicly apologized, and the board accepted his resignation.

Shares fell sharply in the days surrounding the news, with some reports noting double-digit declines from recent highs and substantial market-cap losses. The timing comes just days before Nvidia's scheduled third-quarter earnings on November 17.

## Role of Colette Kress

Kress, who has served as EVP and CFO since 2013, brings deep financial experience from prior roles at Cisco and Microsoft. She has been a steady presence through Nvidia's rapid growth into the dominant AI accelerator provider. Analysts view her as a capable interim leader for continuity, though questions remain about long-term vision without Huang's hands-on involvement in technology and customer relationships.

Huang was known not only for product strategy but also for personally engaging with startups, joining fundraising calls, and helping secure customers—actions that helped fuel the broader AI ecosystem.

## Implications for AI Industry

Nvidia's GPUs and software stack underpin much of current AI training and inference. Leadership stability matters for chip roadmaps, export policies, and partnerships with major AI labs. The board's appointment of a chair for the first time signals a shift toward more formal governance.

Investors and partners will watch the earnings call closely for signals on continuity of product strategy, including next-generation platforms. Asian governments and AI startups that relied on Huang's advocacy for market access have sought reassurances.

## Looking Ahead

The search for a permanent CEO will likely consider candidates with both technical and operational strength. Internal names mentioned in coverage include product leaders with hardware and software experience. External options may also surface given the scale of the role.

This transition occurs amid intense competition in AI accelerators from AMD, Google, Amazon, and others, as well as ongoing debates over export controls. Nvidia's execution in the coming quarters will test whether the company can maintain its momentum without its iconic founder at the top.
