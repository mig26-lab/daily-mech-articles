# Gemini Robotics 2.5: DeepMind Accelerates Physical Intelligence with 96% Factory Success

**Published: November 14, 2026**  
**Category: Robotics / AI**

Google DeepMind has taken a significant leap in embodied AI with the release of Gemini Robotics 2.5 on November 10, 2026. This vision-language-action (VLA) model represents the company's most capable system yet for enabling robots to perform real-world tasks with greater dexterity, speed, and reliability.

## Key Advances

Gemini Robotics 2.5 builds on previous versions by expanding the range of skills robots can learn and the speed of acquisition. DeepMind evaluated the model across more than 200 distinct tasks, including serving food with tongs, tying bows on gift boxes, pipetting liquids, and handling delicate or deformable objects.

Compared to earlier baselines:

- Success rates improved by 30% to 100%, depending on the task.
- Completion times decreased by 20% to 30%.

In laboratory tests, tasks that previous models struggled with or failed entirely now show strong performance. For example, packing pastries with tongs jumped from 5% to 100% success, closing a toolbox lid from 0% to 93%, and untying a ribbon bow from 0% to 70%.

## Factory Proof-of-Concept at Mercedes-Benz

The standout demonstration involved Apptronik’s Apollo 2 humanoid robots on Mercedes-Benz production lines in Kecskemét, Hungary, and Sindelfingen, Germany. The robots performed automotive parts kitting—picking metal brackets from bulk bins and placing them into sequencing trays for vehicle assembly—with a reported 96% success rate.

This proof-of-concept highlights the potential for robots to take on repetitive tasks, allowing human technicians to focus on more complex work. DeepMind notes improvements in physical safety, with 33% more safe completions near obstacles and 43% near people, guided by reasoning models like Gemini 4 Argon.

## Safety and Open Benchmarks

Alongside the model, DeepMind released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open-source benchmark containing hundreds of prompts designed to test whether robots can be tricked into unsafe actions. Evaluations show Gemini 4 Argon achieving high jailbreak resistance (around 98.8–99.5% in reported tests).

The model is currently rolling out to robotics partners including Agile Robots, Apptronik, and Boston Dynamics. There is no public API yet; access is limited, with an early-access pathway for partners.

## Implications for Physical AI

Gemini Robotics 2.5 marks progress toward general dexterity in unstructured environments. By combining vision, language, and action in a unified model, it reduces the need for task-specific programming. Combined with faster training loops powered by frontier models like Argon, development cycles are shortening—DeepMind credits a four-month turnaround for these gains.

As humanoid platforms mature, systems like this could accelerate deployment in manufacturing, logistics, and eventually domestic settings. Challenges remain in long-horizon reliability, energy efficiency, and seamless human-robot collaboration, but the 96% factory result provides concrete evidence of real-world viability.
