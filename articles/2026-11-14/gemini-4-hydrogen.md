# Google Releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen: Faster Everyday AI for Coding and Agents

**Published: November 14, 2026**  
**Category: AI / Models**

On November 12, 2026, Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a speed- and efficiency-focused model designed for everyday coding, debugging, and agentic workflows. It replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash across many Google products and is positioned as the “daily driver” complementing the deeper-reasoning frontier models Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon.

## Performance Highlights

Hydrogen is adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon and offers a 1-million-token context window with flat pricing of $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Google reports:

- Tasks complete about 2.5× faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at the default high thinking level.
- Terminal-Bench score of 57% (vs. 18.2% for 3.8 Flash).
- Score of 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at high (54 at the new xhigh level), slightly above Argon’s 50.
- More than 5× reduction in fabricated claims compared with the prior Flash model.
- Strong results on HLE-Diamond (58.8%) and other agentic and multimodal benchmarks.

It supports four thinking levels: low, medium, high (default), and xhigh, allowing trade-offs between speed, cost, and quality.

## Availability and Integration

Hydrogen rolled out immediately and broadly:

- Gemini API and Google AI Studio (all tiers, with free-tier limits).
- Gemini app and Google Search (for all users).
- Paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Antigravity and other tools.
- GitHub Copilot and other developer environments.

Unlike the more restricted initial releases of Carbon and Argon, Hydrogen prioritizes wide access from day one. It powers faster responses in Search with more visual elements and relevant links.

## Positioning in the Gemini 4 Family

- **Carbon and Argon**: Frontier models for long-horizon, complex reasoning.
- **Hydrogen**: Rapid execution for iterative coding, agent loops, short research, and everyday tasks.

Partners have reported 2× to 2.8× speed improvements and 12–23% token reductions versus 3.8 Flash. The model is closed-weight and served only through Google infrastructure.

## Impact on Developers and Agents

For coding agents and multi-step workflows, the combination of higher Terminal-Bench scores, lower latency, and reduced hallucination makes Hydrogen attractive for production use. It integrates with tools like Antigravity’s Orchestrator for pairing models. Availability in Copilot and VS Code further lowers the barrier for developers.

As agentic systems proliferate, models optimized for speed and reliability (rather than pure frontier capability) will handle the majority of volume. Hydrogen fills that role while remaining competitive on independent intelligence rankings.
