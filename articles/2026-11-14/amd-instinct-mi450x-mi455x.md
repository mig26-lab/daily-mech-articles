# AMD Expands Instinct MI400 Series with MI450X and Claims MI455X Inference Leadership

![Advanced Computing Hardware Concept](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2026/08/15/04/50/04-50-47-38_640.jpg)
*Image: Technology and robotics visualization from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License)*

On November 12, 2026, AMD announced updates to its Instinct MI400 Series of AI accelerators. The company introduced the Instinct MI450X GPU with 288 GB of HBM4 memory and shared measured inference performance results for the higher-memory MI455X on its Helios rack-scale platform.

AMD reported that the MI455X delivered 21,333 tokens per second per GPU on the DeepSeek-R1 model in an MLPerf Inference Offline configuration. The company stated this represented up to 30% higher throughput than Nvidia’s latest published results for its Vera Rubin system. The testing was conducted by AMD Performance Labs.

## Portfolio Details

The MI450X offers the same core compute, memory bandwidth, and scale-up/scale-out connectivity as the MI455X while providing a lower-memory option (288 GB HBM4 versus the larger capacity on the MI455X, reported around 432 GB in some specifications). AMD positioned the MI450X as a more flexible choice for customers whose workloads fit within the reduced memory capacity, potentially improving cost efficiency for certain inference or training deployments on the open Helios architecture.

Both GPUs are supported by AMD’s ROCm software stack. The Helios platform is designed as an open rack-scale solution. AMD also highlighted related offerings such as the MI430X for scientific computing with strong FP64 performance.

## Competitive Context

The announcement arrives amid intense competition in the AI accelerator market. Nvidia has dominated high-end training and inference with its successive generations, while AMD has pursued an open-ecosystem strategy with Instinct and Helios. Independent verification of the exact performance comparison will be important, as the numbers come from AMD’s own testing against published competitor results.

Large AI infrastructure projects, including SpaceX’s planned multi-billion-dollar supercomputing campuses and hyperscaler deployments, continue to drive demand for high-bandwidth, high-capacity accelerators. Memory capacity remains a critical factor for long-context models, multi-agent systems, and large reasoning models.

## Market and Deployment Outlook

AMD stock moved higher following the announcement. Pricing and exact shipping timelines for the MI450X were not detailed in the initial release. Cloud availability for the latest Instinct parts is still ramping, with many deployments expected into 2027.

These hardware advances underpin the broader AI and robotics boom—providing the compute foundation for models that increasingly control physical systems, run agents, and power real-time applications.
