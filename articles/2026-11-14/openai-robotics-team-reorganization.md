# OpenAI Disbands Standalone Robotics Team, Betting General Models Can Drive Robots

![AI Robotics Concept](https://cdn.pixabay.com/photo/2024/09/21/07/41/ai-generated-9063230_640.jpg)
*Image: Futuristic robotic hand from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License)*

On November 13, 2026, Fortune reported that OpenAI has disbanded its stand-alone robotics team and reassigned the researchers to other parts of the company. The move was confirmed by OpenAI to the publication. Robotics research staff now report to Chief Research Officer Mark Chen within the main research organization, while applied robotics and hardware teams have moved into the Applied organization led by President Greg Brockman.

OpenAI described the reorganization as an acceleration of its robotics efforts rather than a retreat. A spokesperson stated that robotics remains a core part of the company’s long-term vision and that the company still targets a public humanoid demonstration in 2027.

## Shift Toward General-Purpose Models

The decision reflects a technical assessment that general frontier models—particularly GPT-6 Astra—can now be applied directly to robot control without specialized robotics-specific models. Earlier in 2026, researchers had explored building on video models such as Sora for physical understanding. Subsequent results with general models changed that calculus.

In an Nvidia study cited in reporting, a GPT-6 Astra-powered agent achieved a 74.8% success rate on a simulated robotics manipulation benchmark, compared with 51% for the best specialized robotics models. Real-world physical results remain more limited; one report noted Astra completing only 7 of 100 bimanual desk tasks in testing versus zero for a specialized competitor, with high per-attempt costs and latency still challenging commercial viability.

This is the second time OpenAI has dissolved a dedicated robotics team. The first was wound down in 2020 amid data limitations. A new team was rebuilt in 2024 and partnered with Figure before that collaboration ended in early 2025.

## Broader Industry Context

The reorganization occurs as physical AI advances rapidly elsewhere. Google DeepMind’s Gemini Robotics 2.5 has reported 96% success rates on live Mercedes-Benz production lines for part-sorting tasks. Companies such as Apptronik, Figure, Unitree, and Agile Robots continue to raise large rounds and pursue factory and warehouse deployments. Tesla has indicated progress toward production-quality Optimus units.

OpenAI’s approach prioritizes leveraging its frontier language and multimodal models as the “brain” for robots rather than maintaining a parallel specialized stack. The empty booth at the recent Conference on Robot Learning underscored the short-notice nature of the change.

## Implications for Embodied AI

If general models prove sufficiently capable and reliable for physical control, the field may see less need for large specialized robotics research groups at frontier labs. However, challenges around latency, cost, sim-to-real transfer, safety, and fine motor control remain significant. Hardware, sensors, and data collection pipelines continue to matter independently of the model architecture.

The move also raises questions about talent allocation and research focus as OpenAI pursues both agentic software systems (including the newly launched ChatGPT Wallet) and physical robotics ambitions.
